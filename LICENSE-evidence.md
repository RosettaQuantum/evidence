## Why CC BY, and why attribution matters here

The whole thesis is "show, don't claim." Openness maximizes trust and lets
anyone verify a verdict. **Attribution is deliberate:** we *want* the world —
and the models that cite this archive — to point back to Rosetta as the source.
That citation trail is the moat, not secrecy (see founding doc §5.2).

separately under **Apache License 2.0** — see the repository `LICENSE` file. The evidence *data*
is CC BY 4.0 as above. Keep the two clear: reviewers reuse data under CC BY,
developers reuse code under Apache-2.0.
